About Us

Klinik Morraz Medicare offers a comprehensive range of medical services focused on the healthcare needs of individuals and families. Our services include general medical consultations, health screenings, vaccinations, and treatment for common illnesses and injuries.

Our motto, "We Care", underscores our commitment to delivering compassionate and attentive healthcare to our patients. This dedication is reflected in personalized care plans, attentive staff, a welcoming environment, and a focus on patient wellbeing beyond just medical treatment.

Mass Screening Services

At Morraz Medicare, we excel in administering comprehensive on-site mass screening services, ensuring the health and safety of various communities and organizations. Our services are designed to detect potential health issues early, promoting proactive healthcare management.

Types of Screenings Offered

  • RTK/PCR Tests: Reliable COVID-19 testing to manage and control the spread of the virus.
  • Medical Assessments: Comprehensive health evaluations conducted by experienced medical professionals.
  • Health Screenings: General health checks, including blood pressure, cholesterol, and glucose levels.
  • 2D Ultrasound Services: Non-invasive imaging for early detection of internal abnormalities.
  • FOMEMA: Managing and monitoring the health screening.

Target Audience

  • Employees: Promoting a healthy and productive workforce.
  • Students: Ensuring early detection and treatment of health issues.
  • General Public: Providing accessible healthcare services to communities.
  • Event Participants: Ensuring health and safety compliance during events.

We have successfully conducted numerous COVID-19 screening events, utilizing RTK and PCR tests. Our services include medical assessments, continuous health monitoring, and on-site testing with strict adherence to safety protocols. Our mass screening services are designed to provide thorough health assessments, ensuring early detection and fostering a healthier community.
